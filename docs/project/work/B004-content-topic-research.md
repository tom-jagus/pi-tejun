# Content-topic research

## Outcome

Find worthwhile leads for the user's public content and interesting technology
anniversaries for a weekly calendarium. Use the user's interests, brand
identity, intended audience, and previous decisions. Keep editorial research and
selections in `content-production`.

## Why

A newsworthy development is not necessarily a useful content lead. Research
should identify why an audience might care, without deciding how the user will
present or prepare a selected topic.

## Boundaries

Pi Tejun owns the repeatable workflow; `content-production` owns the editorial
material and decisions. Consult authoritative brand guidance without creating
another version of the user's identity. Personal memory implementation remains
outside this project.

Research is independent of the digest and does not require a particular content
format. The calendarium may become a weekly video or part of a stream. Do not
turn recommendations into user selections, prepare candidates for presentation,
or publish. B005 owns preparation after selection.

## Acceptance criteria

- Leads identify the subject, supporting sources, audience interest or value,
  and significant uncertainty without assuming popularity proves value.
- Recommendations account for the user's interests, brand, intended audience,
  and known previous content, with missing context explicit. They do not assume
  every audience member has the same technical experience.
- A separate calendarium identifies interesting technology events from the
  current local Sunday-to-Saturday week in previous years, with dates and
  sources. It does not require a lesson or one event for every day.
- Prior selections, declined candidates, and already reported leads inform later
  research. A reconsidered lead explains the material change; unchanged
  pursued leads are not repeated as updates.
- Candidate research, agent recommendations, and user selections remain
  distinguishable in `content-production`. Research failures are visible.
- Research does not commit the user to a format or publication, or start
  preparation without the agreed selection authority.
