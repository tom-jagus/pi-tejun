# Recording analysis and editing assistance

## Outcome

Explore whether analysis of stream recordings can reduce the user's editing
work through timestamped topic discovery, proposed standalone segments, and
reviewable edits. Keep recording-related decisions and preparation material
with the relevant content in `content-production`.

## Why

A stream may contain useful material separated by waiting, repetition, or topic
changes. Assistance should make that material easier to find and reuse without
requiring a full manual review each time.

## Boundaries

This is a later candidate, not a prerequisite for topic research or preparation.
Preserve original recordings and begin by establishing a useful level of
assistance rather than committing to fully automatic finished videos.

Transcripts alone do not capture all screen activity, and silence is not
necessarily removable. Separate suggestions, rough cuts, accepted edits, and
publication. Choose media storage separately; large recordings need not enter
Git. Uploading recordings to external services requires an explicit privacy
and access decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- A representative recording can be navigated through timestamped topic or
  segment suggestions with identifiable supporting evidence.
- Suggested segments explain their value and retain enough context to avoid
  changing the meaning of the original material.
- Editing proposals account for relevant visual activity and useful pauses
  rather than treating transcript gaps as sufficient reasons to cut.
- Any generated edit is reviewable, traceable to the source recording, and
  does not modify or replace the original.
- The user can assess time saved after corrections, not merely whether a
  rendered video was produced.
- No external upload or publication occurs without the separately agreed
  authority for that action.

## Open questions

- What recording formats, languages, durations, and screen content are typical?
- Would timestamps, an edit list, or rough cuts provide the first useful result?
- Which processing tools, hardware, storage, and service privacy terms fit?
- How much manual correction is acceptable before the assistance loses value?
